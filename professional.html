The journey so far...

The FAST Years

  1. July 2019

    The journey begins

    Joined FAST Enterprises

    After graduating from Oregon State University, I was hired as an Implementation Consultant at FAST Enterprises, and I began my professional journey in government software. Eager to work at the state government level, I was excited to learn and grow in this new role.

  2. August 2019

    A big move and a strong start

    Minnesota NMDRIVE Team

    Moving half-way across the country to Minnesota to join the NMDRIVE team, where I started building my development and consulting skills. After being impacted by COVID lockdowns, our team went fully remote and still delivered a system upgrade. Working remotely was new for both me and FAST, but strong collaboration resulted in a successful rollout.

  3. May 2021

    A hop over to the plains

    North Dakota LEGEND Team

    After a successful implementation in Minnesota, I moved to North Dakota to join the LEGEND team to focus on upgrading interface connections to the National Motor Vehicle Title Information System (NMVTIS). I was responsible for moving from batch reporting to real time transmission of title data to jurisdictions across the country to ensure federal compliance.

  4. July 2022

    Off to the northeast

    Vermont VTTRIPS Team

    Next was a move to Vermont to continue my work with NMVTIS development, but also expacnding to credit card interfaces and re-vamping postcard renewals for the state. I was able to take on a leadership role and help guide the project to success.

  5. February 2024

    Away from the snow, into the desert

    New Mexico Tapestry Team

    After looking for opportunities not in northern states, I moved to New Mexico to lead a team during a system-wide code conversion from VB .NET to C#. After 18 months, our rollout was successful and I moved into a production support manager role, being responsible for day-to-day operations and support of the system. Additionally, I help manage yearly legislative changes to ensure the system is always complient as the state requirements evolve.

  6. Current

    Sights set on the future

    Family and Community

    As I close the chapter on my time at FAST Enterprises, I am looking forward to finding career opportunities closer to family and focusing on giving back to my community. While I imagine software development will always be a part of my career, I am looking forward to exploring any new opportunities that may present themselves.
